Phil Taylor, vice-chancellor and president of the University of Bath in the UK, visited the University of Macau (UM) today (18 September) and was warmly received by Yonghua Song, rector of UM. The two sides held in-depth discussions on international academic exchange, research collaboration, and joint talent development programmes, and identified areas for further cooperation. ...
